# Đánh giá tác động của chính sách giảm thuế GTGT đến doanh nghiệp giai đoạn hậu Covid-19 (website)

*Assessing the Impact of the Value-Added Tax Reduction Policy on Enterprises in Post-COVID-19 Vietnam: Empirical Evidence from VES Data*

Static, bilingual (Vietnamese/English) website for the thesis of Trương Nhật Duy (UEH). **Working draft, not peer reviewed.**

- `index.html`: overview, policy phases, interactive results explorer, figures, policy brief, open issues
- `thesis.html`: full text of the thesis body (chapters 1–4 and conclusion), converted from the LaTeX sources; contents panel, equations (KaTeX, self-hosted in `vendor/`), tables, figures, references
- `data/results.json`: headline estimates (author-reported; PSM-DiD and Synthetic DiD marked unverified)

The data appendix is not included. No firm-level data or analysis code is published here; those stay in a private repository. Contact the author for access.
